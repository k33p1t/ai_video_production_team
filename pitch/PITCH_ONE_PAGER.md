# One-Page Pitch  
## The Video Production Factory

**What it is**  
An AI-orchestrated production team that researches rising and niche topics, turns the best ideas into finished videos, checks its own work, and never publishes anything without your explicit final approval.

**The problem**  
Consistent video production is a chain of exhausting steps: spotting trends early, researching without copying, planning, writing, recording, editing, checking rights and quality, publishing, and monitoring. One person doing all of it burns out or drops quality.

**The solution**  
Eight specialized agents + one assembly worker, coordinated by a Supervisor that never does creative work itself:

- **Researcher** finds real signals and evidence  
- **Strategist** turns them into original concepts  
- **Storyboard Creator** plans every shot  
- **Scriptwriter** writes natural spoken language  
- **Voice specialist** delivers clean narration  
- **Assembly worker** builds the actual video  
- **Quality Assurance** catches defects and routes them to the right fixer  
- **Publishing & Monitor** releases only after approval and watches the results  

**The advantage most systems miss**  
A living **Idea Bank** that remembers products, games, tools, clothing, creators, and niches with cult-like or fanboy followings. Agents vote. You vote (your vote counts 3×). Interest slowly decays so the list stays fresh but strong evergreen ideas are not forgotten. The dashboard shows a live Top 20 with full drill-down.

**How quality and safety are handled**  
Every important action is logged permanently. When something fails, the system classifies the problem and sends it back to the exact stage that can fix it — or stops and asks you. There is a hard limit on automatic retries. Rights, safety, and publishing decisions cannot be overridden by a high trend score.

**The non-negotiable rule**  
Nothing becomes public until you approve the exact final video version. If anything changes after approval, the approval is invalidated.

**Why this is different**  
Most automation tries to remove the human. This system removes the exhaustion. Machines handle tireless research, consistent production steps, and careful checking. You keep taste, judgment, risk decisions, and final responsibility.

**One sentence**  
A specialized team that finds the ideas, does the heavy lifting, repairs many of its own mistakes, and still leaves the final “yes” with you.
